# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and remember who Arc is for. Blind and low-vision people are the experts on their own experience; when they say something does not work for them, that is the finding, not an opinion to argue with.

To report behaviour that breaks this, open the repository's **Security** tab and use **Report a vulnerability** (it is a private form that reaches the maintainer), and say it is a conduct report.
